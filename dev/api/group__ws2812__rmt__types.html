◆ Error

enum class ws2812_rmt::Error : uint8_t
strong

Why a call failed. Values 0 to 9 are the shared base set; chip-specific values start at 16.

The pixels have no read-back path: no identity, no status, no CRC, so Timeout, DeviceIdMismatch, Crc, Busy and DeviceFault are never returned (docs/contract.md).

Enumerator
Ok 

The call succeeded.

NotInitialized 

EnsureInitialized() has not succeeded.

InvalidArgument 

Validate() failed, or a pixel index is out of range.

Bus 

A port hook (init, resolution, transmit, power pin) returned false; an underrun the port cannot see is not reported (docs/contract.md).

Timeout 

Not returned by this driver: the port reports a timeout as false.

DeviceIdMismatch 

Not returned by this driver: the pixels are write-only.

Crc 

Not returned by this driver: the frame has no checksum.

Busy 

Not returned by this driver: Show() blocks until the frame is out.

DeviceFault 

Not returned by this driver: no pixel reports a fault.

Unsupported 

PowerOn() or PowerOff() on a port without a GpioSet() hook.

TimingUnreachable 

The port's tick rate cannot place any tick count inside a timing window.

NotPowered 

Show() or TraceWire() between PowerOff() and PowerOn(): nothing is sent.

◆ Variant

enum class ws2812_rmt::Variant : uint8_t
strong

The timing generation (window set) of the part, chosen by what is printed on or known about it.

No single waveform serves every part: the windows of the WS2812B alone differ between the 2013 documents, the 2017 document and the V5 and V6 documents, and their intersection is empty. Each named variant is the intersection of the windows of all documents that describe the same parts, so a waveform inside it satisfies every one of those documents (docs/timing.md).

Enumerator
Ws2812 

WS2812 (6 pin): 0.35/0.8/0.7/0.6 us +-150 ns, reset above 50 us.

Ws2812bPreV5 

WS2812B and WS2812S of 2013 to 2017: reset above 300 us.

Ws2812bV5 

WS2812B V5 and V6, WS2812B-2020, WS2812B-1313, WS2812C-2020: reset above 280 us.

Ws2812c 

WS2812C (5050) and WS2812-2020: T1L window 220 to 420 ns.

Ws2812d 

WS2812D-F8: 750 to 1000 ns on T0L and T1H.

Sk6812 

SK6812 RGB (Rev 01): 0.3/0.9/0.6/0.6 us +-150 ns, reset 80 us.

Sk6812Rgbw 

SK6812RGBW: 32 bit frame, low inside a frame below 20 us.

Sk6812Mini 

SK6812MINI and MINI-E: windows of the later revisions, low below 20 us. The SK6812MINIRGBX sheet (skm) only adds windows: its RGBW part takes Sk6812Rgbw or Custom.

Custom 

Any other part with the same frame: the whole timing is Config::custom.
