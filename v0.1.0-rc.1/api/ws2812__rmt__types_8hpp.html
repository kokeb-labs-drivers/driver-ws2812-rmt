Detailed Description

Public types of the WS2812_RMT driver: errors, Result<T>, colours, the timing profile of every supported part, Waveform and Config.

The parts are single-wire addressable LEDs (WS2812 and WS2812B in every published timing generation, WS2812C, WS2812D, SK6812, SK6812RGBW, SK6812MINI) with one 24 bit (32 bit for RGBW) NRZ frame at about 800 kbit/s: a bit is a high pulse then a low pulse, its value is the length of the high pulse, and a low longer than the reset time latches the frame. The family shares the frame; the timing windows, the reset time, the colour order and the channel count differ, and those are the data of this header (docs/timing.md and docs/datasheet.md give each value its document and page).
